1. Introduction to Computers

Computers are used in education, business, healthcare, science and everyday life.

2. Main Parts of a Computer

CPU: Processes instructions and controls operations.

Monitor: Displays text, images and videos.

Keyboard: Helps enter text and commands.

Mouse: Helps select and interact with items on screen.

RAM: Temporarily stores data currently in use.

Storage: Keeps files and programs for later use.

3. Types of Computers

Desktop: A computer commonly used at a desk.

Laptop: A portable computer with a built-in screen and keyboard.

Tablet: A portable touchscreen computing device.

Supercomputer: A powerful system used for complex calculations.

Server: A computer that provides services or resources to other devices.

4. Uses of Computers

  • Learning and online education
  • Communication through email and messaging
  • Creating documents and presentations
  • Banking and business operations
  • Medical research and hospital management
  • Programming, design and entertainment

5. Advantages of Computers

  • Process many tasks quickly.
  • Store large amounts of information.
  • Help people communicate around the world.
  • Support research, learning and problem-solving.
  • Automate repetitive tasks.

6. Basic Computer Care

  • Keep the computer clean and well ventilated.
  • Save your work regularly.
  • Use strong passwords and keep software updated.
  • Avoid opening suspicious links and attachments.
  • Shut down or restart the computer appropriately.

Conclusion

Computers are important tools in modern life. Understanding their components, types and uses helps us use technology more effectively and safely.
